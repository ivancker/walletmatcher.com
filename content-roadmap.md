# WalletMatcher Content Roadmap

This document tracks future content ideas for WalletMatcher.

Website: https://walletmatcher.com/

## Goal

The goal of WalletMatcher content is to help users choose the right hardware wallet or cold wallet based on their needs, experience level, and crypto storage habits.

WalletMatcher focuses on practical wallet selection, not generic rankings.

## Content Principles

Every WalletMatcher article should:

- Answer the main question quickly
- Compare wallets based on real user intent
- Avoid unnecessary technical complexity
- Include internal links to related comparison pages
- Include a clear quiz CTA
- Include affiliate disclosure where needed
- Avoid exaggerated claims
- Avoid financial advice

## Main SEO Theme

WalletMatcher should be positioned as a decision tool:

> Do not just compare wallets. Match the wallet to the user.
